About

I am a Ph.D. student in Statistics at Arizona State University. My research interests lie in machine learning and computational statistics, with a current focus on generative modeling and uncertainty quantification.

Previously, I worked in Professor Annie Qu’s lab at UC Irvine on mobile data collection and longitudinal health research. I received my M.S. in Electrical and Computer Engineering from UC Irvine and my B.Eng. in Automation from East China University of Science and Technology.

Selected Research

FlowPINN: One-Step Generation

How can generative models produce samples without a long sequence of numerical integration steps? In FlowPINN, we learn neural flow maps using physics-informed residual minimization, enabling generation in a single network evaluation.

Uncertainty Quantification for Flow Matching

Numerical solvers introduce approximation error when sampling from flow-matching models. We use probabilistic numerical methods to quantify this error and refine sample estimates.
