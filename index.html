Selected Research

One-Step Generative Modeling

FlowPINN

Learning neural flow maps through balanced ODE residual minimization at the map’s output to generate samples in one network evaluation.

117× faster sampling than a 100-evaluation flow-matching solver on MNIST using a UNet, with comparable FID (24.29 vs. 24.23) across five seeds. One-step CIFAR-10 FID of 8.69, compared with MeanFlow (9.88) and Shortcut (23.65), using the same backbone and training-step budget.

Uncertainty Quantification

Numerical Uncertainty in Flow Matching

Sampler-agnostic numerical-error bars from each sampler’s Jacobian, with two scale parameters per sampler and step count calibrated offline against probabilistic-solver endpoints. A parallel-in-time probabilistic ODE solver uses a Gauss-Markov prior and Gauss-Newton conditioning across all time nodes in log-SNR time.

Three rounds of probabilistic refinement improved CIFAR-10 FID from 11.05 to 5.13 and halved MNIST median endpoint error from 7.2 to 3.6.
